TruePass – How to use

Make passport, visa and ID photos at home: the right size, no watermark, and your photo never leaves your PC.

1. Take a good photo first

The app can size and tidy a photo, but it cannot fix a bad one. For a photo that will be accepted:

  • Stand in front of a plain, light wall – white or off-white, no pattern, no shadows.
  • Face a window so the light falls evenly on your face. Do not use a lamp from one side.
  • Ask someone to take the picture from about 1.5 m (5 ft) away, at the height of your eyes. Leave space above the head and include the shoulders.
  • Look straight at the camera, mouth closed, neutral expression, eyes open. Take off glasses, hats and headphones unless the rules of your document allow them.

Take several photos and pick the sharpest one. Use the original file, not a small copy sent through a chat app.

2. Open your photo

  • Click Open a photo and choose the picture, or drag the file onto the window.
  • Or click Use camera to take one with your webcam: allow the camera when Windows asks, then click Take photo.

Under the buttons TruePass tells you whether the photo is large enough to print sharply.

3. Choose the document

Under 2 · Document choose what the photo is for, for example United States – passport / visa (2 × 2 in) or Europe / Schengen (35 × 45 mm). The line below shows the exact size and how tall the head must be.

Rules differ by country and document and they change. Always check the official requirements before you send or hand in your photo.

4. Line up the face

The picture on the right shows the finished photo with colored guide lines:

  1. Drag the photo with the mouse to move it.
  2. Use the Zoom slider (or the mouse wheel) until the head is the right size.
  3. Put the top of the hair on the blue line.
  4. The chin must end inside the green band. If it is above the band, zoom in; if it is below, zoom out.
  5. Keep the nose on the dotted line in the middle.
  6. If the head is tilted, use Straighten.

The guide lines are only a help on screen. They are never printed or saved. Untick Show the guide lines to see the photo without them.

5. Improve the picture

  • Brightness and Contrast – small changes only; the face must look natural.
  • Whiten the background – move the slider to the right until the wall turns white. Stop as soon as it does: too far, and light hair or a white shirt may fade into the background.

Whitening only works with a plain light wall. It cannot remove furniture, patterns or strong shadows – take the photo again in front of a better wall.

6. Save the finished photo (free)

Click 💾 Save photo. The photo is saved as a JPG file in your Downloads folder, at the exact size of the document and with no watermark. Use this file for online applications, or take it to a photo shop.

7. Print a sheet of photos (Pro)

  1. Under 4 · Print sheet choose your Paper (for example 4 × 6 in photo paper or A4) and How many photos.
  2. Click the Print sheet tab above the picture to see the page.
  3. Click 🖨 Print sheet. In the Windows print window choose the same paper size and set the scale to 100% or Actual size – not “Fit to page”.
  4. Measure the check line printed at the bottom with a ruler: it must be exactly 50 mm (1.97 in). On sheets without a check line, measure one photo instead.
  5. Cut along the thin grey lines.

No photo printer? Click ☰ More → Save the print sheet as a picture, copy the file to a USB stick or your phone, and print it as one normal 4 × 6 in (10 × 15 cm) photo at a kiosk or shop. Ask for “no cropping, no auto-enhance”.

8. Your own sizes (Pro)

For a document that is not in the list choose My own size… under Document and type the width and height in millimetres. For unusual paper choose My own size… under Paper.

9. Free version and Pro

Free: make and save one finished photo in every standard size – always without a watermark. Pro is a one-time purchase and adds print sheets, saving the sheet as a picture, and your own photo and paper sizes. Click ★ Get Pro at the top to see the price, or Restore purchase if you bought it before.

10. If something goes wrong

  • The printed photo is the wrong size: the printer scaled the page. Print again with Scale 100% / Actual size and check the 50 mm line.
  • I cannot zoom out far enough: the head fills too much of the original picture. Take a new photo from further away.
  • The background does not turn white evenly: the wall has shadows or is too dark. Stand further from the wall and face the light.
  • The camera does not start: close other programs that use the camera and check Windows Settings → Privacy → Camera.
